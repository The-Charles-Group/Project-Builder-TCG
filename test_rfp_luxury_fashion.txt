REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL (RFP)
Digital Marketing Campaign for Luxury Fashion Brand

Project Title: Spring/Summer 2025 Luxury Fashion Digital Campaign
Client: Heritage Luxury Fashion House
Issue Date: October 16, 2025
Response Due Date: November 1, 2025

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Our luxury fashion house is seeking a premier digital marketing agency to develop and execute a comprehensive digital marketing campaign for our Spring/Summer 2025 collection launch. This campaign will introduce our new sustainable luxury line to high-net-worth consumers across North America and Europe.

PROJECT SCOPE
The selected agency will be responsible for:

1. Digital Strategy Development
   - Consumer insights and market research
   - Competitive analysis of luxury fashion digital campaigns
   - Digital roadmap for SS25 collection launch
   - Omnichannel strategy integration

2. Creative Campaign Development
   - Campaign concept and creative direction
   - Visual identity for digital channels
   - Luxury brand storytelling narrative
   - Content creation for all digital platforms

3. Social Media Marketing
   - Instagram, TikTok, and Pinterest strategy
   - Influencer partnerships with luxury fashion influencers
   - User-generated content campaigns
   - Social commerce integration

4. Digital Advertising
   - Programmatic display advertising
   - Social media advertising campaigns
   - Google Ads and shopping campaigns
   - Retargeting and lookalike audiences

5. Content Production
   - Fashion photography and videography
   - 360-degree product photography
   - Runway show digital coverage
   - Behind-the-scenes content creation

6. Email Marketing
   - VIP customer segmentation
   - Personalized email campaigns
   - Automated customer journeys
   - A/B testing and optimization

7. Website Enhancement
   - Landing page design and development
   - E-commerce optimization
   - Mobile experience enhancement
   - Virtual try-on technology integration

8. Performance Analytics
   - KPI dashboard creation
   - Weekly performance reports
   - ROI analysis
   - Customer journey mapping

TARGET AUDIENCE
- Primary: High-net-worth individuals aged 25-45
- Secondary: Luxury fashion enthusiasts and collectors
- Geographic focus: New York, Los Angeles, Paris, Milan, London

CAMPAIGN TIMELINE
- Campaign Planning: December 2024 - January 2025
- Creative Development: January - February 2025
- Pre-Launch Activities: March 2025
- Main Campaign Launch: April 2025
- Campaign Duration: April - August 2025
- Post-Campaign Analysis: September 2025

BUDGET RANGE
$2.5 million - $3.5 million USD

EVALUATION CRITERIA
Proposals will be evaluated based on:
- Previous luxury fashion brand experience (30%)
- Creative portfolio quality (25%)
- Strategic approach and innovation (20%)
- Team expertise and credentials (15%)
- Cost effectiveness (10%)

DELIVERABLES REQUIRED
Please include in your proposal:
- Agency credentials and luxury fashion case studies
- Proposed campaign strategy and creative concepts
- Detailed timeline with milestones
- Budget breakdown by deliverable
- Team structure and key personnel bios
- Three client references from luxury brands

SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS
- Proposals must be submitted in PDF format
- Maximum 50 pages including appendices
- Include visual examples of previous work
- Provide pricing in USD

Thank you for your interest in this project. We look forward to reviewing your innovative proposals for our Spring/Summer 2025 luxury fashion campaign.

END OF RFP
