REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL
MAISON LAURENT PARIS
Spring/Summer 2025 Seasonal Campaign

OVERVIEW
Maison Laurent Paris, a premier luxury fashion house established in 1947, seeks an agency partner for our Spring/Summer 2025 global campaign launch. With heritage rooted in Parisian haute couture and a modern vision for sustainable luxury, we require comprehensive creative and strategic services for our most ambitious seasonal launch to date.

CAMPAIGN OBJECTIVES
- Launch SS25 Ready-to-Wear collection across 42 global markets
- Elevate brand perception among affluent millennials and Gen Z (ages 25-40)
- Drive 40% increase in e-commerce revenue during campaign period
- Strengthen positioning as leader in sustainable luxury fashion
- Generate earned media value of $50M+

SCOPE OF WORK REQUIRED

1. STRATEGIC PLANNING
- Consumer insights and market analysis for luxury fashion segment
- Competitive landscape assessment (Chanel, Dior, Saint Laurent)
- Brand positioning refinement and messaging architecture
- Integrated campaign strategy across all touchpoints
- Sustainability narrative development

2. CREATIVE DEVELOPMENT
- Campaign concept and creative platform
- Art direction for all visual assets
- Video production for hero campaign film (3-5 minutes)
- Photography for print and digital (minimum 200 shots)
- Motion graphics and CGI for digital experiences
- Runway show creative direction for Paris Fashion Week

3. DIGITAL & SOCIAL
- Social media strategy across Instagram, TikTok, WeChat, and emerging platforms
- Influencer and celebrity partnership strategy
- Content creation calendar (6 months)
- AR/VR experiences for virtual try-on
- Website refresh and e-commerce optimization
- Email marketing campaigns

4. TRADITIONAL MEDIA
- Print advertising for Vogue, Harper's Bazaar, WSJ Magazine
- Out-of-home installations in key cities (Paris, NYC, Tokyo, Shanghai)
- Retail window displays and in-store experiences
- Direct mail for VIP clientele

5. EVENTS & EXPERIENCES
- Paris Fashion Week runway show production
- Press preview events in 10 cities
- VIP customer experiences and trunk shows
- Pop-up installations in luxury department stores
- Influencer trips and brand immersions

6. PUBLIC RELATIONS
- Global PR strategy and execution
- Press release development and distribution
- Media relations and press outreach
- Crisis communication planning
- Thought leadership development for CEO

7. PARTNERSHIPS & COLLABORATIONS
- Celebrity brand ambassador negotiations
- Artist collaboration curation
- Luxury hotel and travel partnerships
- Cultural institution partnerships

DELIVERABLES TIMELINE
- January 2025: Strategy and creative concept presentation
- February 2025: Campaign asset production begins
- March 2025: Paris Fashion Week show
- April 2025: Global campaign launch
- May-June 2025: Sustained campaign activation
- July 2025: Campaign wrap and performance analysis

BUDGET
Total campaign budget: $15-20 million USD
Agency fees expected: $2-3 million USD

SELECTION CRITERIA
- Proven luxury fashion experience with global brands
- Strong creative portfolio with award-winning work
- Digital innovation and emerging technology capabilities
- Sustainability credentials and purpose-driven approach
- Global network with local market expertise
- Senior team involvement and dedicated resources

SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS

- Proposed strategic approach
- Initial creative concepts
- Team structure and bios
- Project timeline and milestones
- Detailed cost breakdown
- References from luxury clients
