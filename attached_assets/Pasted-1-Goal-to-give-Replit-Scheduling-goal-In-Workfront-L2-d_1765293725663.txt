4. What ASAP will do (answering your concern)

You can tell them explicitly:

If they only change ConstraintType on deliverables to ASAP but don’t add dependencies, Workfront will try to start all those deliverables on the project start date → bad.

With the FS predecessor chain you’re asking for:

Deliverable 1 starts at the compressed project start.

Deliverable 2 starts As Soon As Possible after Deliverable 1 finishes, because of the FS dependency.

Deliverable 3 starts after Deliverable 2, and so on.

So “ASAP + dependencies” = the waterfall you want; “ASAP without dependencies” = everything stacks at day one.
