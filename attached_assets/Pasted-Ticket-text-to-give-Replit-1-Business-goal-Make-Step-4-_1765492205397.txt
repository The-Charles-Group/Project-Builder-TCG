2. Current behavior (what’s wrong)

In Step 4, on the deliverable card:

Brand Identity Development shows
Span: 92 calendar days (2026‑01‑05 – 2026‑04‑06)
Effort days @ 8h/d ≈ 39 days

The 92‑day span comes from earliest_start → latest_end of the timeline tasks (calendar days).

The 39 “effort days” is just 307 hours ÷ 8 h/day (front‑end calc).

In the MSP/XML export, the L2 deliverable “Brand Identity Development” is written with:

<Start>2026‑01‑05T08:00:00</Start>

<Finish>2026‑04‑06T17:00:00</Finish>

<Duration>PT43680M</Duration> (43680 minutes ≈ 91 calendar days at 480 min/day)

When importing that XML into Workfront:

The L2 deliverable is a summary task with Work=PT0M, DurationFormat=7 and (in tight‑waterfall mode) auto‑scheduled with ASAP/SNET constraints.

Workfront ignores the 91‑day Duration for the summary and recalculates it from the child tasks, which are chained finish‑to‑start and sum to 26 working days.

That’s why Workfront shows 26 Days, Start 1/5/26, Due 2/11/26, while the app shows a 92‑day span to 4/6/26.

The backend already has a “business‑day” view of each deliverable, but it’s not surfaced in the UI or used consistently in the export:

compress_deliverable_timeline computes duration_business_days for each deliverable by counting business days between its earliest and latest internal task dates (Mon–Fri only).

It then builds a compression_map[code] with:

new_start, new_end

duration_business_days (this is the number we want to match Workfront)

shift_days used to move the deliverable tasks forward/back.

That metadata is saved under result['metadata']['compression'] and then into scenario['timeline_metadata'].

/api/timeline/save currently computes project total_duration_days purely from calendar earliest_start/latest_end and stores it in scen["timeline"]["duration_days"].

Step‑4 UI appears to be using that calendar data + a local effort calculation.

6. About constraints in the XML

Yes, the constraints and DurationFormat in the XML absolutely influence how Workfront behaves, but only up to a point:

Root task: ConstraintType=4 (Must Start On) and Duration=PT0M.

L2 deliverables in tight‑waterfall:

Type=1 (Fixed Duration), IsEffortDriven=0, ManuallyScheduled=0, constraints SNET/ASAP for the chain, and DurationFormat=7.

Leaf tasks have explicit Start/Finish and Durations based on actual timestamp differences; they obey the Mon–Fri calendar.

Because L2 deliverables are summary tasks with auto‑scheduling, Workfront recalculates their Finish/Duration from the children and its Standard calendar, effectively overriding any calendar‑based Duration we send. That’s exactly why Workfront shows 26 days even though the XML said 91 days.
