Why Workfront is rejecting this XML

1) Dangling predecessor references (the big one)

Your XML contains <PredecessorLink> entries where:
	•	a task says it depends on PredecessorUID = 4001, 8003, 13004, etc…
	•	but there is no <Task><UID>4001</UID></Task> anywhere in the file

That’s a hard integrity failure for some importers (and Workfront often throws the generic “unable to read” toast when it hits this).

Example pattern I saw repeatedly:
	•	Task UID 5 depends on PredecessorUID 4001
	•	Task UID 9 depends on PredecessorUID 8003
	•	…and so on

This strongly suggests your exporter is generating “composite” predecessor IDs (or keeping old UIDs) but later renumbering tasks or filtering tasks out, without remapping predecessors.

2) Duration vs Start/Finish inconsistency (causes “0.5 days”, wrong end dates, and rescheduling)

In multiple exports you’re setting task Duration based on Work (hours), even when the task Start/Finish span is much longer.

Workfront/MSP importers will often “pick a truth”:
	•	either trust Start/Finish and recompute Duration
	•	OR trust Duration and recompute Finish
When they don’t match, you get exactly what you’re seeing: shortened durations, shifted end dates, weird fractional days.

If you want Workfront to keep your dates, your XML must satisfy:
	•	Duration == working_time_between(Start, Finish) (in minutes on your calendar)
	•	Work == planned effort (also minutes)

Work can be smaller than Duration (that represents “waiting window”), but Duration must match the Start/Finish span.
