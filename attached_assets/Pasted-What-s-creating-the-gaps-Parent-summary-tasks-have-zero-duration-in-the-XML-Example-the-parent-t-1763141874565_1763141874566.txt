What’s creating the gaps

Parent (summary) tasks have zero duration in the XML
Example: the parent task “Creative Strategy / Campaign Plan Deck” is exported with Duration=PT0M (zero) while also carrying Start=2026‑05‑14 and Finish=2026‑09‑22. Workfront doesn’t honor a parent’s explicit Finish when Duration is zero, so it falls back to a short default window, which is why it shows ~5 days instead of spanning the children.

Tasks aren’t chained with predecessors; they’re pinned by constraints
I don’t see any <PredecessorLink> entries in this export. Instead, tasks are anchored with constraints (e.g., “Brand Positioning Strategy” is ConstraintType=2 “Start No Earlier Than” with ConstraintDate=2025‑11‑17). Without FS predecessors, Workfront doesn’t flow tasks together—it just drops each group on its own constraint date, leaving gaps.

Calendar mismatch encourages unexpected spacing
Your XML calendar sets Saturday as a working day (09:00‑12:00 and 13:00‑18:00). If the Workfront schedule for your environment is Monday–Friday, the importer remaps durations to a 5‑day week and the re‑calculation can shorten spans and create apparent gaps between groups.
