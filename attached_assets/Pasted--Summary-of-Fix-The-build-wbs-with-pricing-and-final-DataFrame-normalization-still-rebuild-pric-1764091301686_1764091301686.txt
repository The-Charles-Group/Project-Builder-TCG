🔧 Summary of Fix

The build_wbs_with_pricing() and final DataFrame normalization still rebuild prices from hours_by_role (row_price = hours × rate).
That’s why your exports ignore Step 3 overrides (Planned_Hours + Price_USD).

The fix has three parts:

Scale hours_by_role to match Step 3’s Planned_Hours.

Preserve Step 3’s Price_USD (and use it as FixedCost) instead of recomputing.

Skip double-counting by zeroing assignment cost when FixedCost is set.
