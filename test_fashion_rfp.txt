LUXURY FASHION BRAND CAMPAIGN BRIEF

Client: Maison Élégance (Luxury Fashion House)
Campaign: Spring/Summer 2024 Collection Launch

OVERVIEW:
Maison Élégance seeks a comprehensive agency partner for the global launch of our Spring/Summer 2024 ready-to-wear collection. The campaign must embody our heritage of French craftsmanship while appealing to a new generation of luxury consumers.

KEY DELIVERABLES REQUIRED:

1. SEASONAL CAMPAIGN DEVELOPMENT
- Full creative concept for SS24 collection
- Mood boards and visual direction
- Campaign narrative aligned with "Modern Heritage" theme
- Cross-channel content strategy

2. FASHION WEEK ACTIVATION
- Paris Fashion Week runway show production
- Livestream and virtual showroom setup
- Press day coordination and management
- Backstage content capture and distribution

3. LOOKBOOK & EDITORIAL
- 40-look editorial shoot production
- Lookbook design and production (print & digital)
- E-commerce product photography
- Behind-the-scenes video content

4. INFLUENCER PARTNERSHIPS
- Identify and secure 5 global brand ambassadors
- Negotiate celebrity endorsement deals
- Micro-influencer seeding program (50 influencers)
- Content creation guidelines and contracts

5. EXCLUSIVE EVENTS
- Flagship store opening gala in Paris
- VIP trunk shows in NYC, London, Milan
- Press preview and buyer appointments
- Pop-up experiences in key markets

6. DIGITAL EXPERIENCE
- Virtual fashion show production
- AR try-on experience development
- Luxury e-commerce platform optimization
- NFT collection for exclusive pieces

7. HERITAGE STORYTELLING
- Brand heritage documentary series
- Atelier craftsmanship content
- Founder story campaign
- Museum partnership activation

TIMELINE:
- Campaign kick-off: January 2024
- Fashion Week show: March 2024 (Paris)
- Collection launch: April 2024
- Campaign duration: 6 months

BUDGET CONSIDERATIONS:
- Premium talent and celebrity endorsements required
- Exclusive venue rentals for events
- High-end production values essential
- Multiple rounds of creative refinement expected

TARGET AUDIENCE:
- Primary: HNW individuals aged 25-45
- Secondary: Emerging luxury consumers in Asia
- Influencer community and fashion press

SUCCESS METRICS:
- Brand awareness lift in key markets
- Social media engagement and reach
- Press coverage value
- Sales performance vs. previous season

This campaign requires an agency with deep luxury fashion expertise, established relationships with top-tier talent, and the ability to execute flawlessly across multiple touchpoints. The selected agency must understand the nuances of luxury positioning and deliver work that meets the exacting standards of haute couture.
