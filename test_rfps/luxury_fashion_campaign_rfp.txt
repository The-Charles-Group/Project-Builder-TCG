LUXURY FASHION BRAND GLOBAL CAMPAIGN RFP
==========================================

CLIENT: Maison Étoile Paris
PROJECT: Spring/Summer 2026 Collection Launch
DATE: October 2025

BRAND OVERVIEW:
Maison Étoile is a heritage French luxury fashion house established in 1952, known for haute couture and ready-to-wear collections. We are seeking a creative agency to develop and execute our Spring/Summer 2026 global campaign.

CAMPAIGN OBJECTIVES:
- Reinforce brand positioning as timeless French elegance
- Introduce new creative director's vision
- Drive 40% increase in global sales
- Expand presence in Asian markets
- Attract younger luxury consumers (25-35)

SCOPE OF WORK:

1. CREATIVE CONCEPT DEVELOPMENT
   - Campaign theme and narrative
   - Visual identity and mood boards
   - Key art direction
   - Brand storytelling

2. PRODUCTION
   - Campaign photoshoot (Paris and Tokyo)
   - Video content (hero film + social cuts)
   - Behind-the-scenes content
   - Model casting and styling
   - Location scouting

3. INFLUENCER PARTNERSHIPS
   - Identify and secure 10 global ambassadors
   - Exclusive influencer events
   - Content creation partnerships
   - Celebrity dressing opportunities

4. DIGITAL EXPERIENCE
   - Interactive lookbook
   - Virtual showroom
   - AR try-on features
   - Shoppable content

5. FASHION WEEK ACTIVATIONS
   - Paris Fashion Week runway show production
   - Press and buyer presentations
   - VIP experiences
   - Pop-up installations

6. RETAIL INTEGRATION
   - Flagship store windows (15 locations)
   - In-store experiences
   - Sales associate training
   - Clienteling tools

DELIVERABLES:
- Complete creative concept presentation
- 50+ campaign images
- 5 hero videos
- Social media content library (100+ assets)
- Influencer content packages
- Digital lookbook
- Retail rollout materials

TIMELINE:
- Creative concept approval: November 2025
- Production: December 2025 - January 2026
- Paris Fashion Week: February 2026
- Global launch: March 2026
- Campaign duration: March - August 2026

BUDGET: €1.8M - €2.2M

TARGET MARKETS:
Primary: France, Italy, UK, USA, China, Japan
Secondary: Middle East, South Korea, Russia

MANDATORY REQUIREMENTS:
- Previous luxury fashion experience
- Global campaign capabilities
- Offices in Paris and major markets
- Fashion week production expertise
