HEALTHCARE DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION RFP
======================================

ORGANIZATION: MedTech Innovations Healthcare System
PROJECT: Patient Experience Digital Platform
DATE: October 2025

BACKGROUND:
MedTech Innovations is a network of 12 hospitals and 45 clinics seeking to revolutionize patient care through digital innovation. We need a partner to design and implement a comprehensive digital health platform.

PROJECT GOALS:
- Improve patient satisfaction scores by 30%
- Reduce appointment wait times by 50%
- Increase telehealth adoption to 60%
- Streamline clinical workflows
- Ensure HIPAA compliance

SCOPE OF WORK:

1. PATIENT PORTAL DEVELOPMENT
   - Appointment scheduling system
   - Medical records access
   - Prescription management
   - Bill pay integration
   - Secure messaging with providers

2. TELEHEALTH PLATFORM
   - Video consultation infrastructure
   - Remote patient monitoring
   - Digital triage system
   - E-prescriptions
   - Integration with EHR systems

3. MOBILE HEALTH APPS
   - iOS and Android native apps
   - Symptom checker
   - Medication reminders
   - Health tracking integration
   - Emergency services locator

4. CLINICAL DECISION SUPPORT
   - AI-powered diagnostic assistance
   - Treatment recommendation engine
   - Drug interaction checker
   - Clinical pathway optimization
   - Predictive analytics dashboard

5. DATA INTEGRATION
   - EHR/EMR integration (Epic, Cerner)
   - Laboratory systems integration
   - Pharmacy systems connection
   - Insurance verification
   - Health information exchange

COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENTS:
- HIPAA compliance
- HITECH Act requirements
- FDA regulations for medical devices
- State healthcare regulations
- ADA accessibility standards

DELIVERABLES:
- System architecture design
- User interface mockups
- Functional prototypes
- Production systems
- Integration documentation
- Training programs
- Maintenance plan

TIMELINE:
- Discovery phase: 2 months
- Design phase: 3 months
- Development: 8 months
- Testing and validation: 2 months
- Phased rollout: 3 months
- Total: 18 months

BUDGET: $4M - $5M

EVALUATION CRITERIA:
- Healthcare industry experience
- Technical capabilities
- Compliance expertise
- Implementation methodology
- Post-launch support

SUBMISSION DEADLINE: November 15, 2025
