REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL
GLOBAL LUXURY FASHION CONGLOMERATE DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION

CLIENT: Étoile Luxury Group
DATE: October 15, 2025
BUDGET: $25-35M
TIMELINE: 24 months

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Étoile Luxury Group, the world's third-largest luxury conglomerate managing 12 iconic fashion houses including Maison Lumière, Casa Bellezza, and Atelier Royale, seeks a transformative agency partnership for comprehensive brand elevation, digital innovation, and market expansion across our entire portfolio.

This RFP outlines requirements for a multi-year engagement encompassing creative excellence, strategic innovation, technological advancement, and sustainable luxury practices across 120+ global markets.

COMPANY BACKGROUND
Founded in 1923, Étoile Luxury Group has grown from a single Parisian atelier to a global powerhouse with:
- Annual revenue: €28 billion
- 12 luxury fashion houses
- 2,800+ retail locations worldwide
- 450,000 employees globally
- Presence in 120 countries
- 35 million active customers
- 15 manufacturing facilities
- 8 design studios

Our portfolio spans:
- Ready-to-wear collections
- Haute couture
- Leather goods and accessories
- Jewelry and watches
- Fragrances and cosmetics
- Eyewear
- Home and lifestyle products

STRATEGIC OBJECTIVES
1. Position Étoile as the definitive leader in sustainable luxury
2. Capture next-generation luxury consumers (Gen Z and Alpha)
3. Expand digital revenue from 15% to 40% by 2027
4. Establish dominance in emerging luxury markets
5. Create industry-leading metaverse experiences
6. Pioneer circular economy in luxury fashion
7. Achieve carbon neutrality by 2030
