Media Agency Request For Proposal - Uncommon Schools

About: Non-profit network of 52 outstanding public charter schools in grades K-12 across New York, New Jersey, and Massachusetts. Mission to close the opportunity gap and prepare students from low-income backgrounds for college success.

Objectives:
• Enhance Brand Awareness & Perception: Achieve 40% increase in high-quality leads for teacher recruitment and student enrollment over 5 years
• Drive Enrollment & Retention: Attain over 95% enrollment capacity for new schools and 85% year-over-year persistence rates by 2030
• Measure & Optimize Campaign Effectiveness: Establish real-time analytics dashboard with 20% improvement in media spending efficiency

Target Audience:
• Parents of preK-12 students (Black, Hispanic, Low/Moderate Income)
• PreK-12 teachers and operations staff
• Markets: Brooklyn, Rochester, Camden, Newark, Boston

Current Media Channels:
• Meta, Google Search, YouTube/Streaming TV, Streaming audio
• Programmatic web ads, Out of Home, Direct Mail
• Email Marketing, LinkedIn, Indeed

Scope of Work:
• Develop comprehensive paid media strategy
• Execute multi-channel campaigns
• Market research and audience insights
• Creative campaign development
• Performance optimization and reporting
• Integration with internal teams
• Technical requirements for tracking and analytics
