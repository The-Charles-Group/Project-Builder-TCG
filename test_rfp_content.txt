UNCOMMON SCHOOLS MEDIA AGENCY RFP - MAY 2025

ABOUT UNCOMMON SCHOOLS:
- Non-profit network of 52 public charter schools (K-12) across NY, NJ, and MA
- Mission: Close opportunity gap for low-income students
- 25-year history, 20,000 students, 85% economically disadvantaged
- 5 regions: Brooklyn, Rochester, Newark, Camden, Boston
- Students graduate college at 5x rate of peers

PROJECT OVERVIEW:
We need a media planning agency to develop, execute, and optimize comprehensive paid media strategies aligned with our 5-year strategic plan to re-establish Uncommon as the nation's best PK-12 schools.

KEY OBJECTIVES:
1. ENHANCE BRAND AWARENESS & PERCEPTION
   - Goal: 40% increase in high-quality leads for teacher recruitment and student enrollment over 5 years
   - Establish Uncommon as gold standard in charter education

2. DRIVE ENROLLMENT & RETENTION
   - Goal: 95% enrollment capacity for new schools
   - 85% year-over-year persistence rates by 2030

3. MEASURE & OPTIMIZE CAMPAIGN EFFECTIVENESS
   - Real-time analytics dashboard within first year
   - 20% improvement in media spending efficiency within 2 years

TARGET AUDIENCES:
- Parents: Black/Hispanic, low/moderate income with children aged 5-15
- Teachers: College degree required, career-changers and recent graduates

CURRENT MEDIA CHANNELS:
Paid: Meta, Google Search/Ad Grant, YouTube/Streaming TV, Streaming audio, Programmatic, Out of Home, Direct Mail, Email, Indeed, LinkedIn
Owned: Social Media (FB, IG, YT, LinkedIn), Website, Email/CRM, School buildings
Earned: News coverage, Op-Eds

SCOPE OF WORK REQUIRED:
- Comprehensive media strategy and planning
- Creative campaign development
- Multi-channel execution (digital, traditional, emerging)
- Performance measurement and optimization
- Market research and insights
- Budget management and allocation
- Integration with internal marketing team
- Regular reporting and analytics
- Testing and innovation strategies

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS:
- Real-time campaign tracking
- Attribution modeling
- Marketing automation integration
- Data privacy compliance
- Cross-channel analytics

CHALLENGES:
- Limited brand awareness in certain regions
- Intense competition for students and staff
- Mature, crowded charter school category
- Demographic shifts affecting enrollment
